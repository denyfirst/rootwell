      04 / MANUAL ASSOCIATION

      Owner is your manual note, not verified ownership. Leave the new owner blank to clear the note. This does not remove the certificate.

      06 / MANUAL LOCATION NOTES

      Change server notes

      These are your notes, not verified deployments. Changing or removing a note does not change the certificate on a server. Older backups can retain the previous note.

      07 / EXPLICIT RECORD DELETION

      Remove from saved list

      This removes the public certificate and its owner/location notes from the current encrypted inventory only. It does not revoke the certificate or remove it from a server. Older snapshots may restore it; this is not secure erasure. Re-import is possible if you still have the public certificate file.

      Backup is manual

      Changes are not automatically backed up.

      How to make a backup

      After any change, stop Rootwell and run rootwelld inventory-snapshot with a new backup filename. Verify and store the snapshot separately from your recovery code. An older snapshot may restore records or notes you later deleted.

      Compare a replacement certificate

      The saved public snapshot is read from your Rootwell. Your candidate stays in this browser. Nothing is replaced or saved; matching names or keys do not prove trust.

        How background checks work

        Closing this page does not stop server checks. Logout, session expiry (up to 12 hours), restart or unavailable storage pauses them. Sign in again to unlock; no password is stored on disk for monitoring.

        Change history

          Not an independent audit log. Earlier activity may be unknown. Deleted fingerprints remain here and in older backups. At 1024 events, changes are refused; nothing is silently removed.
